# Example image provenance

Landscape and portrait were created on 2026-11-20 with the built-in image generation tool. They depict synthetic scenes, not documented real people or locations. The user requested the look of casual phone photographs. Earlier rejected generations are not included.

The interface image is the user's DevNotes screenshot. The illustration is the user's supplied GitHub avatar, used as the existing illustrated example; its original authorship/license was not independently verified. Example images and their reproductions are excluded from the code's MIT license; no separate reuse license is granted here.

## Landscape prompt

An ordinary realistic smartphone photo taken on a sunny summer walk beside a lake. Bright but natural green meadow in the foreground with scattered pink-purple wildflowers and a few small yellow flowers, clear blue-green lake in the middle distance, irregular green wooded hills on the far shore, blue sky with small white cumulus clouds. Cheerful colorful summer daylight, no sunset. Slightly imperfect casual handheld framing, natural phone-camera sharpness and exposure, realistic grass and foliage detail, no artificial HDR, no cinematic grading, no postcard-perfect symmetry, no neon colors. A believable photo someone sends a friend from a walk. Wide horizontal composition. No text, watermark, frame or palette. Example for a color palette extraction project.

## Portrait prompt

Ordinary candid smartphone photograph of a fictional adult woman about 27 sitting at a small cafe table, photographed by a friend at eye level. Dark brown slightly messy shoulder-length hair, natural face with slight skin redness and under-eye texture, relaxed half-smile, casual burgundy sweatshirt. Head and shoulders with a little table visible at bottom, blue-gray cafe wall and a plant softly but not excessively out of focus behind her. Mixed ordinary daylight from a nearby window and warm overhead cafe light, imperfect handheld framing, everyday phone-camera perspective, no portrait-mode fake bokeh, no studio light, no makeup campaign, no retouching, no cinematic color grade, no flawless skin, no glossy fashion look. Portrait vertical photo. Believable spontaneous picture sent in a chat. No text or watermark. Intended as a generated example for a color palette extraction tool.
